Privacy Policy

King Zarry AI Discord Bot

1. Introduction

King Zarry AI ("we", "us", "our", or "the Bot") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the Bot, why we process it, and how we handle it. Please read this policy carefully.

2. What Information We Process

When you use King Zarry AI, we process the following types of information:

Discord User Information

Discord User ID: Your unique Discord user identifier, used to associate commands and requests with your account

Username and Display Name: Your Discord username or server nickname, used to personalize responses and logs

Command and Interaction Data

Slash Command Usage: Records of which commands you execute (e.g., /textvideo, /imagevideo)

Command Parameters: The text prompts and descriptions you provide to the Bot

Timestamps: When commands are executed

Content You Provide

Text Prompts: Any text descriptions you submit for processing or media generation

Uploaded Images: PNG, JPEG, or WebP image files you upload for analysis or video generation

Image Metadata: Technical information about uploaded images needed for processing

Technical and Log Information

Error Logs: Information about any errors or failures during command execution

Request Metadata: Technical details about API calls and interactions

3. Why We Process This Information

We process the information listed above for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To execute your commands and provide the requested features (text processing, image analysis, media generation)
  • Content Processing: To transmit your prompts and images to AI and media providers to generate requested outputs
  • Error Resolution: To diagnose and fix technical issues or errors
  • Service Improvement: To understand how the Bot is used and identify opportunities for enhancement
  • Compliance: To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse

4. Third-Party Services and Data Transmission

To provide its functionality, King Zarry AI transmits certain information to third-party AI and media providers as needed:

Discord

Your Discord user ID and username are processed by Discord as part of normal bot operation. See Discord's Privacy Policy for details on how Discord handles this data.

Groq (Language Model Processing)

When you submit text prompts or request image analysis, your text and image data may be transmitted to Groq's servers for processing by their language models. Groq processes this data according to their own privacy policy and terms of service. We recommend reviewing Groq's privacy documentation for details on their data handling practices.

Hugging Face (Media Generation)

When you request video or media generation, your text prompts and uploaded images are transmitted to Hugging Face for processing and generation. Hugging Face processes this data according to their own privacy policy and terms of service. We recommend reviewing Hugging Face's privacy documentation to understand how they handle your data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

King Zarry AI uses a combination of temporary processing and persistent storage for operational purposes. Here's what happens to your data:

Temporary Data Processing

  • Text Prompts: May be processed in-memory during request execution; temporary copies may be retained during processing
  • Uploaded Images: Temporary copies may be created for processing; these temporary files are cleaned up after the request completes
  • Generated Media: Video and media files are generated on-demand and sent to Discord; temporary processing files are cleaned up after transmission

Persistent Storage

  • Discord User IDs and Usernames: May be retained in logs and databases for account association and service functionality
  • Command History and Logs: Records of commands executed, timestamps, and parameters may be retained for error diagnosis, service improvement, and compliance purposes
  • Persistent Memory/User Context: Where applicable, the Bot may maintain persistent memory of user interactions, preferences, or conversation history to improve service functionality
  • Error Logs and Metadata: Technical logs related to errors, API requests, and system performance may be retained for debugging and service optimization

Important: Specific retention periods are not guaranteed. Data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide service, comply with legal obligations, or for legitimate operational purposes. Third-party services (Groq, Hugging Face) have their own data retention policies; refer to their documentation for details on how long they retain your data after processing.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • Secure transmission of data to third-party services
  • Secure handling of API credentials and tokens
  • Cleanup of temporary processing files after use

However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data, particularly when transmitted to third parties or processed by external AI providers.

7. Your Rights

Regarding your data:

  • You can stop using the Bot at any time
  • You can request information about what data we process about you
  • For data stored by third parties, you should review their privacy policies for your rights regarding their stored data

To exercise these rights or request information, please contact us through Discord or GitHub.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 is using the Bot, we will take appropriate steps to remove their information and restrict their access.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the Bot following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated privacy policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Through Discord
  • Via GitHub repository issues or discussions

11. Third-Party Links and Services

This Privacy Policy applies only to King Zarry AI. If you access third-party services through our Bot (Discord, Groq, Hugging Face, etc.), their privacy policies govern how they handle your information. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies independently.
