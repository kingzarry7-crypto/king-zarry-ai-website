Privacy Policy

King Zarry AI Discord Bot

1. Introduction

King Zarry AI ("we", "us", "our", or "the Bot") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the Bot, why we process it, and how we handle it.

2. What Information We Process

When you use King Zarry AI, we process the following types of information:

Discord User Information

Discord User ID: Your unique Discord user identifier, used to associate commands and requests with your account

Username and Display Name: Your Discord username or server nickname, used to personalize responses and logs

Command and Interaction Data

Slash Command Usage: Records of which commands you execute (e.g., /textvideo, /imagevideo)

Command Parameters: The text prompts and descriptions you provide to the Bot

Timestamps: When commands are executed

Content You Provide

Text Prompts: Any text descriptions you submit for processing or media generation

Uploaded Images: PNG, JPEG, or WebP image files you upload for analysis or video generation

Image Metadata: Technical information about uploaded images needed for processing

Technical and Log Information

Error Logs: Information about any errors or failures during command execution

Request Metadata: Technical details about API calls and interactions

3. Why We Process This Information

We process the information listed above for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To execute your commands and provide the requested features (text processing, image analysis, media generation)
  • Content Processing: To transmit your prompts and images to AI and media providers to generate requested outputs
  • Error Resolution: To diagnose and fix technical issues or errors
  • Service Improvement: To understand how the Bot is used and identify opportunities for enhancement
  • Compliance: To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse

4. Third-Party Services and Data Transmission

To provide its functionality, King Zarry AI transmits certain information to third-party AI and media providers:

Discord

Your Discord user ID and username are processed by Discord as part of normal bot operation. See Discord's Privacy Policy for details.

Groq (Language Model Processing)

When you submit text prompts or request image analysis, your text and image data may be transmitted to Groq's servers for processing by their language models. Groq processes this data according to their Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Hugging Face (Media Generation)

When you request video or media generation, your text prompts and uploaded images are transmitted to Hugging Face for processing and generation. Hugging Face processes this data according to their Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The King Zarry AI Bot does not persistently store your content by default. Here's what happens to your data:

  • Text Prompts: Processed in-memory during request execution; not permanently stored by the Bot itself
  • Uploaded Images: Processed during the request; temporary copies may be created for processing, then deleted
  • Generated Media: Video and media files are generated on-demand and sent to Discord; temporary files are cleaned up after transmission
  • Command Logs: Basic execution logs may be maintained for error diagnosis and service improvement, but are not indefinitely retained
  • Third-Party Processing: Third-party services (Groq, Hugging Face) have their own data retention policies; refer to their documentation for details on how long they retain processed data

Important: While the Bot itself does not permanently store your content, the third-party services used for processing may retain copies according to their own policies. You should review their privacy policies directly.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted transmission of data to third-party services
  • Secure handling of API credentials and tokens
  • Immediate deletion of temporary files after processing

However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data when transmitted to third parties.

7. Your Rights

Regarding your data:

  • You can stop using the Bot at any time
  • You can request information about what data we process about you
  • For data stored by third parties, you should review their privacy policies for your rights regarding their stored data

To exercise these rights or request information, please contact us through Discord or GitHub.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 is using the Bot, we will take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the Bot following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Through Discord
  • Via GitHub repository issues or discussions

11. Third-Party Links and Services

This Privacy Policy applies only to King Zarry AI. If you access third-party services through our Bot (Discord, Groq, Hugging Face, etc.), their privacy policies govern how they handle your data. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
